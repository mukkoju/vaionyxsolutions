About VAIONYX

Accessible skill assessment, built for learners

VAIONYX Solutions is an online platform focused on short skill assessments and digital certificates.

Our purpose

VAIONYX Solutions aims to give learners, students, fresh graduates, developers, and technology professionals a simple way to test and demonstrate their knowledge through focused online assessments.

We are building a digital experience that is simple to access, straightforward to complete, and clear about what a certificate represents.

What we value

Simple

A clear journey from choosing an assessment to viewing the result.

Accessible

An online format designed to be available wherever users can access the platform.

Affordable

Assessment fees are shown for each service when offered and may change over time.

Digital

Assessment and eligible certificate delivery are handled electronically.

What our certificates mean

A VAIONYX Solutions certificate confirms successful completion of the corresponding VAIONYX Solutions skill assessment. VAIONYX Solutions is not a university, college, government certification authority, or government-approved certification body. A certificate is not an academic degree and does not guarantee employment or acceptance by an employer or institution.

Assessment topics may include cloud computing, artificial intelligence, Scrum, software development, and other technology-related skills. The available catalog can change as the platform develops.
