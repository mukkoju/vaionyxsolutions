The assessment journey

Choose → Assess → Evaluate → Pass → Certificate

Each assessment listing should explain its subject, format, applicable fee, and passing criteria before purchase.

STEP 01

Choose

Select an available certification assessment.

STEP 02

Review

Read its description, fee, and assessment details.

STEP 04

Assess

Answer the assessment questions online.

STEP 05

Evaluate

The submitted answers are checked against the passing criteria.

STEP 06

Certificate

If you pass, your digital certificate is made available electronically.
